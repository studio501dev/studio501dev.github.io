Nocturne Bébé

White noise, heartbeat, rain, lullabies: 12 sounds to soothe your baby. No ads, fully offline.

Privacy policy

A steady breath, a heartbeat, rain on a window: Nocturne Baby plays the soft, continuous sounds little ones know well. Mix them, set each volume and start everything with one gesture. The app is designed for parents.

Sounds are generated by the app itself: everything works offline, even in a room without network. For your baby’s safety, keep the phone at least 2 metres from the cot, at a moderate volume.

Nocturne Baby gives no medical advice and does not claim to treat any condition. One-time purchase, no subscription; Google Play offers updates when the app opens.

Key features

What you will appreciate

  • ✓12 sounds to mix, each with its own volume: white, pink and brown noise, heartbeat, womb, rain, waves, fan, hair dryer, car ride, music box, whisper
  • ✓Ready-made ambiences
  • ✓Your favourite mix saved, with a shortcut to the last sound
  • ✓Timer, or all-night playback
  • ✓Playback with the screen locked, with controls in the notification
  • ✓Soft night light with adjustable brightness
  • ✓Safety reminders: distance from the cot and moderate volume
  • ✓No ads, no account, no data collected, fully offline
  • ✓10 languages: French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Indonesian, Japanese and Korean
