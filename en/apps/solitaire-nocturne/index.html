Solitaire Nocturne

Classic Klondike with an option for winnable deals. No ads, no account, fully offline.

Privacy policy

Solitaire the way you like it, finally without ads. Green felt, handsome cards, smooth drag and drop: Solitaire Nocturne lets you play in peace, with no pop-ups, no account and no connection.

With the “winnable deals” option, a deal is checked for a possible win before it is offered to you. Get a hint when you are stuck, and undo a move if you regret it.

One-time purchase, no subscription. Google Play offers updates when the app opens; the game stays playable offline.

Key features

What makes the difference

  • ✓“Winnable deals” option: a solution exists before the game starts
  • ✓Draw 1 or draw 3
  • ✓Smooth drag and drop
  • ✓Hint and single-move undo
  • ✓Automatic finish once the win is secured
  • ✓Statistics and best times per draw mode
  • ✓Night, Day or Automatic theme, adjustable sound and vibration
  • ✓No ads, no account, fully offline
  • ✓10 languages: French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Indonesian, Japanese and Korean
