Aquarevane

Eighteen illustrated puzzle games, 60 guided journeys and a keepsake journal to take offline.

Privacy policy

Aquarevane offers 1,080 puzzles across six illustrated destinations. Voyage mode guides you through 60 journeys of 18 challenges: one level from each game, then the next journey. Challenges already completed in free play count towards the voyage.

Prefer to choose? All 18 games and their six chapters are open in free play. Find a game with search and favourites. Each game keeps one active puzzle per mode, so switching between Voyage and free play preserves the other puzzle.

Your first victory in each game reveals one of 18 keepsakes. Complete at least one level in each of the three games on an island to earn its postcard, with six to collect. Levels, artwork and the journal are bundled with the app and remain available in airplane mode.

A privacy-conscious approach

Privacy policy

Puzzles in both modes and your progress stay on your device. Keepsakes and postcards are derived from your locally saved victories. Studio501 does not receive your gameplay data; Google Play handles the technical information required for updates.
