Aurélia — Les Jardins oubliés

Merge enchanted objects and bring forgotten gardens back to life. Offline, with no ads or energy timer.

Privacy policy

A seed becomes a shoot, then a flower. Merge two matching objects into something more precious: every creation brings a forgotten garden closer to life.

Restore each location, discover item families and grow your workshop. Short orders, rare creations and special dispensers let you decide how to use the space on your board.

The game interface is in French. Planned one-time price: €1 in France, with no ads, subscription or in-app purchases. No energy timer: play at your own pace, even offline. Requires Android 8 or later and an up-to-date Android System WebView or Chrome.

Key features

Everything you need, clearly.

  • ✓Drag-and-drop or tap-to-select merging
  • ✓Eight gardens to restore over time
  • ✓Six item families and rare alchemical creations
  • ✓Rebuild special dispensers while keeping their products
  • ✓Orders, permanent upgrades and challenges
  • ✓Local saves with progress export and import
  • ✓Built-in sound, animations and settings
  • ✓No ads, account, subscription or in-app purchases
