Accordo

Tune at a glance. Guitar, bass, ukulele, violin. No ads, works offline.

Privacy policy

Tune quickly, play in tune. Accordo brings a tuner and a metronome together: a clear needle, simple settings and no ad popping up at the wrong moment.

The microphone is used only to listen to your instrument: sound is analysed on your phone, never recorded, never sent.

One-time purchase, no subscription. Google Play offers updates when the app opens; the app works without a connection.

Key features

The tuner and metronome in your pocket

  • ✓Chromatic, 6- and 7-string guitar, 4- and 5-string bass, ukulele, violin, viola and cello
  • ✓Alternate tunings and savable custom tunings
  • ✓Automatic string detection or lock the string you want
  • ✓Reference notes to tune by ear, adjustable reference pitch
  • ✓Metronome from 20 to 300 BPM, tap tempo
  • ✓Time signatures with adjustable accent on each beat, and subdivisions
  • ✓Several click sounds or vibration only
  • ✓The metronome keeps playing with the screen locked
  • ✓No ads, no account, works offline
  • ✓10 languages: French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Indonesian, Japanese and Korean
