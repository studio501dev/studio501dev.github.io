Pairline

Find strictly identical copies and review which ones to keep.

Try on Microsoft StorePrivacy policy

Add folders to scan, exclude or protect. Pairline confirms duplicates by comparing their content byte by byte.

Scanning moves no files. Selected copies are revalidated before a confirmed request to move them to the Windows Recycle Bin. V1 scans validated local NTFS locations and excludes network, cloud and removable locations.

Free full one-day trial through Microsoft Store, then a one-time purchase: €1 in France, with Microsoft showing the local currency price elsewhere. No subscription or automatic charge. Access to app features stops when the trial expires unless you choose to buy.

Key features

Everything you need, clearly.

  • ✓Folders to scan, exclude or protect
