02Local operation

The app is designed to work offline, with no Studio501 account, advertising, marketing analytics, tracking SDK or Studio501 server. It does not request the Internet permission.

03Data processed on your device

The app stores locally your games in progress, your statistics and best times per draw mode, and your preferences (theme, sound, vibration, “winnable deals” option). This information is used only to run the game.

04Collection and sharing by Studio501

Studio501 does not receive, collect, sell or share any game data. The app requests no sensitive permission.

05Purchase and app updates

Purchase and licensing are handled by Google Play, separately from the app. Studio501 receives no payment data.

When the app opens, it automatically checks with Google Play whether an update is available. Google documents that this service processes device metadata, the app version and installed modules to determine update availability and size. This information is encrypted in transit and processed under Google Play policies. This feature does not transmit anything entered or produced in the app.

You can postpone installation. The app remains usable offline and on devices without Google Play.

06Retention and deletion

Data stays in the app’s private storage until you edit or delete it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall it.

07Support, rights and changes

If you contact support, your email address and message are used only to reply and follow up; they are not sold or used for advertising. You may ask for access, correction or deletion of this information at contact@studio501.fr.

Solitaire Nocturne is not specifically directed at children. This policy will be revised if permissions, dependencies or processing change.
