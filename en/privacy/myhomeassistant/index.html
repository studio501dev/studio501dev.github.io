01Controller

This policy applies to MyHomeAssistant, published by Studio501. Contact: studio501.dev@gmail.com or contact@studio501.fr.

02Data processed

The app requires no account and does not transmit entered data to a Studio501 server.

  • home-maintenance tasks and tracked equipment;
  • due dates, notes, descriptions and checklists;
  • action history;
  • local language, theme, notification and widget preferences.

03Local storage

Data is stored locally on the phone or tablet. It is not sent automatically to a developer server.

If a backup or export is used, the generated file remains under the user’s control and the user chooses where to save or share it.

04Notifications

Reminders are generated locally on the device and do not require sending tasks to an external server.

05Widget

The widget displays upcoming or overdue tasks using local app data.

06Google Play updates

The app may use Google Play services to check whether an update is available. Google Play manages this check under its own rules.

07Advertising and tracking

MyHomeAssistant contains no integrated advertising and uses no advertising-tracking tool.

08Sharing

Studio501 does not share entered data. It leaves the device only after a voluntary user action, such as exporting a backup or sharing a file.

09Security

Processing is local. Users remain responsible for the security of their device, backups and exported files.

10Deletion

Data can be deleted in the app, by resetting app data through Android, or by uninstalling. Exported files must be deleted separately.

11Children

MyHomeAssistant is not specifically intended for children and does not provide child-directed content.

12Changes

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The date at the top identifies the most recent version.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact Studio501: contact@studio501.fr.

studio501.dev@gmail.com also remains available for existing Store listings.
