01Controller and scope

This policy applies to Decision Tree Studio for Windows, published by Candela — Studio501, France.

For privacy or support questions, contact studio501.dev@gmail.com or contact@studio501.fr.

02Personal data

Decision Tree Studio does not require a user account. Studio501 does not collect projects, decision trees, content or files created in the app.

03Local operation

Projects remain on the device unless the user explicitly chooses to export or share them.

04Telemetry and advertising

Decision Tree Studio contains no advertising and is not designed to send personalised usage or telemetry data to Studio501.

05Storage, backups and deletion

App data and backups are stored locally. Users control their external exports and backups. Private app data can be removed by deleting projects or uninstalling the app according to Windows behaviour.

06Support

Information voluntarily provided by email is used only to respond and follow up on the request. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact Studio501: contact@studio501.fr.

studio501.dev@gmail.com also remains available for existing Store listings.
