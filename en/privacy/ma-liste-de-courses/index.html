01Controller

This policy applies to Ma Liste de Courses, published by Studio501. Contact: studio501.dev@gmail.com.

02Plain-language summary

The app works locally on the device. It requires no account, contains no advertising or advertising tracking, and does not send shopping lists to a Studio501 server.

03Data collection

Studio501 does not collect or share personal data for analytics, advertising or profiling.

When the official Google Play In-App Updates system is used, minimal technical data may be processed by Google Play to check for an update and estimate its size. This may include device metadata, app version and installed modules. Google Play controls this processing.

04Data stored on the device

The following information is stored locally to provide the app features:

  • shopping lists, items and quantities;
  • checked and unchecked states;
  • categories, icons and remembered corrections;
  • display preferences, theme and language.

05Manual sharing

A list is exported or shared only after an explicit user action through Android features. Data then goes to the app or location chosen by the user, not to a Studio501 server.

06Backup and automatic transfer

The app is configured not to use automatic Android cloud backup. Backup and restore functions provided by the app are initiated and controlled by the user.

07Retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until it is changed or deleted, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Exported files must be deleted separately from their saved location.

08Security

Lists remain stored locally. Technical exchanges required by the official Google Play update mechanism are encrypted in transit according to Google Play operation.

09Children

The app is not designed specifically for children and Studio501 does not collect personal data through it.

10Changes

This policy may be updated if features change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
