02Local progress

Progress, objects, settings and records are kept in the app’s private storage on your device. No Studio501 account, advertising SDK, advertising or usage analytics is included. Game resources are bundled with the app; no remote game code is downloaded.

03Purchase, updates and Internet access

Google Play handles the one-time purchase. Studio501 does not receive your payment card details. The game contains no in-app purchases or subscription.

The Internet permission is declared. Game resources stay local. Google Play In-App Updates checks for newer releases. Google states that this service processes device metadata, the application version and installed modules or asset packs to determine update availability and size. These exchanges are encrypted and governed by Google Play policies. They do not include game progress. Updates can be postponed, and the game stays playable offline.

A recent Android System WebView or Chrome component is required. If yours is too old, the app offers to open its Google Play update page.

Google Play update service data information

04Backups and selected files

You may export and import a JSON save using Android’s file picker. It contains game progress and settings. You choose the destination, including any third-party service whose own policies then apply.

Android backup of WebView data is enabled. Depending on device and backup settings, Android or Google may back up and restore the app’s data. This operates separately from Studio501 and is controlled in Android settings.

05Retention and deletion

Local data remains while you use the game. Clear the app’s data in Android settings to remove it. Uninstalling removes private storage; exported files and Android backups are managed separately. Studio501 cannot restore progress it does not hold.

06Support, rights and changes

If you contact support, your email and message are used only to reply and follow up for as long as needed. They are not sold or used for advertising. Contact contact@studio501.fr to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us.

The game is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not ask players for personal information. Material changes to these practices will be published here.
