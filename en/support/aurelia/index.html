Support — Aurélia — Les Jardins oubliés

Answers to common questions and support contact.

Application pagePrivacy policySupport Studio501

01How do I merge?

Drag an object onto its match, or tap to select two matching objects. Their family and rank must match. The result takes one cell.

02My board is full

Complete an available order, merge matching objects or use short orders to free space. Products from a special dispenser remain when you rebuild that dispenser.

03How do I keep my progress?

The game saves locally. Export a JSON backup in Settings before changing devices or clearing data. Keep it somewhere you trust, then import it on the other device.

04The game asks for a WebView update

Update Android System WebView or Chrome through Google Play, then reopen Aurélia. This is Android’s game display component, not an additional purchase. Once installed, the game remains playable offline.

05Need help?

Email contact@studio501.fr with the phone model, Android version, Aurélia version and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.
