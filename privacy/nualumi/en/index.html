Privacy policy
Nualumi

The game runs on your device, with no account, ads, advertising tracking or usage analytics.

Updated: October 10, 2026

Publisher and scope

This policy covers Nualumi, the Android coloring game with application ID app.studio501.nualumi.

Nanouk Candela, sole trader. Trading name: Studio501. 61 rue de Lyon, 75012 Paris, France. Contact: contact@studio501.fr.

Data stored locally

In its private app storage, Nualumi keeps colors, progress in each mode, modification dates, framing, favorites, the last picture opened, journeys and earned rewards, language and game preferences.

This information stays on your device. No Studio501 server receives it. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location or advertising identifier.

Network and third-party services

The full collection is included in the app. The game does not request Android’s Internet permission and sends no statistics, game reports or creations to Studio501, advertisers or analytics services.

Google handles purchases, downloads and updates distributed through Google Play under its own terms. Nualumi has no in-app purchases and does not receive your payment information in the game.

This policy describes the app. Google Play, your email provider and file providers used separately have their own privacy practices.

Exports and imports you choose

You can export a PNG image or JSON backup. Android’s document picker lets you choose the destination. Nualumi does not request general access to your files.

If you select a cloud provider in the picker or later share a file with another app, the transfer depends on your choice and the service involved.

A backup may contain your progress, favorites, history and preferences. Only the selected file is read to validate and restore compatible data. Merging keeps the latest compatible picture saves; the device’s existing preferences are retained.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in the app until deleted. You can restart a picture in the game. To remove all local data, use the app’s Android settings or uninstall Nualumi.

Automatic Android backup and transfer of app data are disabled. Before uninstalling or changing devices, export a backup and keep it somewhere accessible.

Exported PNGs and backups stay in your chosen location and must be deleted separately. Studio501 has no copy of your pictures and cannot recover lost progress without a backup.

If you contact support

If you choose to contact Studio501, your email address, message and attachments you send are used to answer and follow up on your request. These exchanges are separate from local game data.

They are kept for as long as needed to handle and follow up on your request and are not used for advertising. Send only information relevant to the problem.

To request access to, correction of or deletion of information sent to support, email contact@studio501.fr.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s operation changes, this page will be updated with a new date. Changes to data practices will also be reflected in Google Play disclosures.
